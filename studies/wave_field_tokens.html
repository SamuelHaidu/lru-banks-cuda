Tokens as radio stations: emit waves, listen with band-pass filters

Each token broadcasts a mix of damped waves on shared "stations" (frequencies), with its own strength, phase and propagation length: its embedding is that signal. The field at a position is everything broadcast before it. Each token also has a receiver, a band-pass filter per station; what it picks up from the field is its contextualized feature. "time" after "Once upon" hears the opening stations; after "took some" it hears others, so it becomes a different vector. Distance lives in the wave phases (no RoPE), and the field is a running sum, so inference keeps a constant-size state. Signals are synthetic, designed to show the mechanism.

Constant-size state at inference the field as a running sum

Fk,l(i) = e−αl + iωk · Fk,l(i−1) + ctok i, k · [tok i in class l]

Each step multiplies the stored field by one complex number per (station, decay class) and adds the new word's broadcast. State = stations x decay classes: here 16 x 3 = 48 complex numbers, whatever the length of the text. The running sum and the explicit sum give the same field: -.

This needs the decay to come from a small set of classes (tokens choose one, or a mix): one accumulator cannot hold signals that fade at arbitrary per-token rates. Training is still parallel: the field for all positions is a causal sum of damped waves (a scan, or our block-banded trick).

What this replaces

Embedding table → emission table: each token stores its broadcast (complex amplitude per station) and its decay class. RoPE → the stations: eiωΔ is the distance code. Banks → decay classes: how long a word's signal carries is chosen by the word. Router / final state → receivers at every position: every word gets a contextualized feature, not just the last one.

Known relatives: complex diagonal state-space models (S4D, LRU) are a field of damped waves with input-independent decay; Mamba makes emission and readout depend on the token. The difference here: the decay belongs to the emitting word (its propagation length), the idea behind the per-token λ of the banks.

Depth: layer-2 words can broadcast from their contextualized features, so later layers listen to compositions ("a character was introduced, then a place"), still parallel and still a constant state per layer.
