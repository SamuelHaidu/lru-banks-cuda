Why this report

`query-recall-trace` used hand-crafted name-recall prompts and found the softmax retrieval step near-uniform for every one of them. This report samples real (context, target) pairs directly from the training corpus -- the same random-document, random-cut-point scheme the model was trained with -- across three context-length buckets ({{ buckets | join(', ') }}), and separates cases the model gets exactly right (target is its own top-1 prediction) from cases it gets badly wrong (target falls outside its own top-10), so both successful and failing retrieval can be compared on ordinary text, not just names.
