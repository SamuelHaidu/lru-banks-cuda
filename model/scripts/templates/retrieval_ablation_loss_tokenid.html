Why this report

The attention/retrieval traces showed the softmax retrieval step has collapsed to a near-uniform, input-independent vector, and that individual token ranks barely move when it is substituted away. This report makes the causal claim directly, in the unit that actually matters -- validation loss over {{ "{:,}".format(num_examples) }} real examples -- under three conditions, with every other trained weight held fixed:

conditionwhat changes
normalthe real forward pass, unmodified
uniformretrieved vector replaced by the exact vector a perfectly uniform vocabulary-weight vector would produce (in-distribution, but query-independent)
shuffledeach example gets another example's real retrieved vector (on-distribution -- a genuine retrieved output -- but mismatched content, isolating "wrong content" from "off-distribution")
randomretrieved vector replaced by genuine random noise (off-distribution; destroys retrieved content entirely)

The comparison that matters is uniform vs. shuffled vs. random: all three remove the query-specific retrieved content, but only random also pushes Block 2 and the FFN readout off the input distribution they were trained on. If uniform and shuffled stay close to normal while random is far worse, the large Δ under random reflects distributional shock, not lost retrieved information -- i.e. the model has learned to rely on the retrieved block having a specific statistical shape (unit-norm, near the big table's mean), not on its query-specific content. If shuffled is much worse than uniform despite both being in-distribution, that would instead mean the retrieved content's specific identity (not just its shape) matters.
