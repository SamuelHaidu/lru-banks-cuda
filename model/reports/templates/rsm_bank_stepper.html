bank

Loss per word

Cross entropy of each word given everything before it (memory reset at the marker): green = expected, red = not. Only the tokens the model is trained to predict are colored (with loss spans: the assistant's turns, its calls and the closing tag); grey = context it reads but does not predict (tool list, user, tool results). Underlined = written by the model itself (after the prefill). Click a word to step to the position that had to predict it.

Read: attention over past tokens

The read ot = Σb rtb bankb / Σb rtb zb is exactly a weighted average of past values: token j gets atj = Σb rtb (1-μb) μbt-j wjb / Σb rtb zb (sums to 100%). Purple = weight. Hover a token for its weight and the banks it came through; click it to select its main bank. The gate g scales the read before it adds to the state.

read over time: at every step (column), the attention weight of each past token in the pooled read (same colors as a legend below; grey = all others). Hover for the read at a step, click to go there.

Banks at this token

All banks, ordered by learned half-life (short left, long right). Up (blue): this token's write softmax wtb. Down (purple): the read's share from each bank, rtb zb / Σ r z (what the read actually got from it; the read softmax rtb itself is the grey outline). Each half scaled to its own largest bar. Click a bank.

Banks over the text

Rows: banks by half-life (short at the top), columns: tokens. Darker = larger (square-root scale, max of this text and layer). Dashed: the current token and the selected bank. Click to go to a token and bank.

What the selected bank stores after this token: each past token's share cjb / zb = (1-μb) μbt-j wjb / zb of the bank's value average (sums to 100%). Click a token to step there.

read from this bank at this token

The part of the pooled read that came through this bank: rtb cjb / Σb' rtb' zb' per past token (these add up to the bank's read share; over all banks they add up to the attention above). Green = weight.

contents over time: at every step (column), the share of each past token in this bank; colored = the tokens that ever reach the largest shares, grey = all others, white = nothing written yet. Hover for the contents at a step, click to go there.
over the text: write weight wtb (blue bars) and written mass zb (black), each on 0..its max in this text (left labels); read share rtb zb / Σ r z (purple) on 0..100% (right). The share is relative: a bank with little mass can take most of the read when the banks the read selects are all nearly empty.

State

The state st after this token (it addresses the read; st-1 addresses the write): each token's share ⟨st, cj⟩ / |st|2, cj = γ λt-j Rt-j uj. Purple adds, red cancels (rotated out of phase).
